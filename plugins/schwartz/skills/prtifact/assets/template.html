Free shipping looks at the total after the coupon.

  1. Free shipping starts at $50. A $60 cart has a $15 coupon. Which side of the line is it on?
  2. The old code looks at the cart before the coupon.
  3. The customer pays $45 and still ships free.
  1. Now the coupon comes off first.
  2. $45 is what gets compared with $50.
  3. Under the line, so the order pays shipping.

A coupon can take off the whole cart and no more.

  1. A $20 cart has a $25 coupon. What does the customer pay?
  2. The old code takes off the whole coupon, so the total is minus $5.
  3. An order can't cost less than nothing.
  1. Now the coupon is capped at what is in the cart: $20 of the $25.
  2. So the total comes to $0.
  3. The rest of the coupon is simply not used.
